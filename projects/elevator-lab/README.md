# Elevator lab

Open this project in the native editor. The lobby lift has Ground, Floor 1 and
Annex buttons. Call it from either floor, walk into the cabin and use E on a
cabin button. Annex loads the second level into its lift; Lobby returns.

The lab opts into checkpoints. Save at a settled landing; saves during travel
are refused. It is an explicit small validation project, outside release-map
preparation. See [authoring and validation](../../docs/ELEVATORS.md).
