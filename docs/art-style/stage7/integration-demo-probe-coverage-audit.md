## Phase selection without weaker support

The global centred spacing is max(1.5,per-axis extent/64); a long world can put lattice X/Z sites outside narrow clear corridor ranges. The correction shares the exact strict air/floor/ceiling/5cm-clearance/opaque-geometry predicate between phase scoring and final labels. Already-covered grids remain bit-identical. For actual misses, up to 63 alternative XYZ quarter-cell phases are counted completely, maximizing covered rooms then balancing sorted per-room populations. A chosen change recovers a room and retains every previously covered room; ties retain the earlier phase. Finding one site per room does not stop balancing. Infeasible overlapping ownership remains a truthful warning.

Targets, baking and serialized field use the selected exact positions. Radius, axis/total caps, opaque visibility, room/source semantics, energy and PLPF v3 zero-source alignment are unchanged. An initial boundary-only candidate failed the real runtime witness and was rejected; source score alone was not accepted as a native pass.
