Original Places lonely tonal interiors (2026-11-07)
Threshold Air, Pale Signal and Below the Landing: original offline single-pitch
synthesis, seeded air and circular room processing; no recordings or third-party
music. Repository MIT license. Recipe: tools/audio/tonal.py.
Original 48 kHz PCM24 masters and the unchanged 44.1 kHz Empty Room source are
preserved under assets/references/audio/tonal_ambient/. Runtime PCM16 formats,
including the reversible narrow-band Empty Room conversion, are explicitly
hash-pinned per file in authoring.json and provenance.json.
