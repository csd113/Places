MIT KEMAR measured head-related impulse responses
Bill Gardner and Keith Martin, MIT Media Laboratory, 1994.

Places uses the complete diffuse-field equalized 128-tap responses (44.1 kHz), resampled offline to 140 taps at 48 kHz using a 32-point Hann-windowed sinc. Azimuths 0..180 are mirrored by exchanging ears. Coordinates: +X right, +Y up, +Z forward. Linear weighted interpolation among three nearby measured directions happens on the game thread; coefficients crossfade in the callback. No personalized HRTF or below -40 degree measurement is claimed.
